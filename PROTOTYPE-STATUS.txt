ABU OTUTO — version 5 prototype, 11 November 2026

Continues the existing version 3 reader and scanned assets.

Implemented and checked with application-logic tests:
- Home screen first; search by hymn number, English title or Igbo first line.
- 32 categories transcribed from the printed Order of Hymns.
- Igbo and English alphabetical sorting.
- Favorites saved in the current browser, with removal and reload checks.
- Full hymn index is presented in clear 50-hymn ranges, with Previous and Next controls; all 294 indexed hymns are accessible.
- Existing animated hymn navigation, grouped continuation scans, separated shared-page hymn views, original scan mode, zoom and title corrections.
- All 356 original page images retained; 294 hymn records mapped.

PWA implementation added: standalone manifest, icons and service worker.
The app shell and viewed pages are cached. This is not a full offline download of all hymns. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted. Favorites and corrections remain on the device and do not sync.

OPENING:
Extract the complete ZIP. Open index.html to use the local reader.
For installation and service-worker caching, serve this folder through HTTPS (or localhost for development). Opening a file directly does not enable PWA features. Install options depend on the browser.

VERIFICATION LIMITS:
Application-logic tests passed. Browser preview did not open successfully; layout, actual animation, touch gestures, installability and offline operation have not been verified in a browser. No live site was published: the connected Sites service returned unavailable.

CONTENT LIMITS:
Igbo titles remain draft transcriptions pending fluent-language proofreading. Optional lyric search uses uncorrected OCR. Read the scans for original lyrics and solfa notation. Hymn 295 appears in the printed index but its song scan is absent; it cannot be supplied from this PDF.

The included REVIEW-NOTES.txt describes the earlier version's content review and source gaps. Its old feature and publication status is superseded by this file.
