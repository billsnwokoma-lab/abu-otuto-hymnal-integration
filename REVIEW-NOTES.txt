CONTENT REVIEW — 3 NOVEMBER 2026

Complete: 356 page images retained; hymn numbers 1–294 mapped to their starting PDF pages; English heading titles compared visually with the scans; all 294 Igbo index first lines transcribed with visual comparison; title and optional draft lyric search added.

Search uses the transcribed title fields. The original scan is always the reading source. Search tolerates omitted diacritics, punctuation and apostrophes, but it cannot compensate for every OCR or transcription error.

OCR observations: The PDF contains only scanned images, with no embedded text layer. OCR confuses dotted vowels, Q/o, mb/m, and musical sol-fa marks with text. Obvious title errors were corrected by comparison with the printed index. The Igbo title transcription remains a draft pending fluent-language and diacritic review; lyric text remains raw OCR and is optional in search. No numerical OCR accuracy percentage is claimed because no independently proofread ground-truth text was supplied.
